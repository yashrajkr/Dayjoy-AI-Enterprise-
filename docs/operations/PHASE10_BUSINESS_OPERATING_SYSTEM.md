# MODULE 4: Pricing Strategy

## Pricing Philosophy

We use a **per-conversation + platform subscription** hybrid model. This aligns our revenue to the value we deliver (conversations handled) while maintaining a predictable base (platform subscription). Unlike per-seat models (Salesforce, Microsoft), our model scales with usage — customers pay for AI-handled work, not for assigning seats to humans who may not use the platform.

# MODULE 5: Sales System

## Sales Playbook

### Sales Motion: Direct (Mid-Market) + Partner-Led (Enterprise)

**Mid-Market (500-5,000 employees)**
- **Cycle**: 60-90 days
- **ACV**: $18K-$60K
- **Motion**: Inside sales + SE (remote demo, POC, close)
- **Decision Makers**: Head of CX, Head of Support, CTO
- **Champion**: Director of Operations, Innovation Lead

**Upper Mid-Market (5,000-25,000)**
- **Cycle**: 90-180 days
- **ACV**: $48K-$200K
- **Motion**: Field sales + SE + CSM (on-site demo, POC, procurement)
- **Decision Makers**: COO, CTO, VP Customer Experience
- **Champion**: Head of Digital Transformation, Head of AI

### Discovery Call Guide (30 minutes)

**Minutes 0-5: Introduction**
- "Tell me about your role and what your team is responsible for."
- "What made you interested in exploring AI for your customer operations?"

**Minutes 5-15: Current State Pain Discovery**
- "Walk me through your current inbound support process — what happens when a customer calls/chats?"
- "What's your average speed to answer? First-call resolution rate?"
- "How many agents do you have? What's your monthly conversation volume?"
- "What happens when call volume spikes 3×? How does your team handle it?"
- "How do you measure support quality today? What's your CSAT?"
- "What does after-hours coverage look like?"
- "What's your cost per conversation today?"

**Minutes 15-22: Vision & Impact**
- "If AI could handle 60% of conversations, what would your team focus on?"
- "What's the cost of NOT deploying AI in the next 12 months?"
- "Who else needs to be involved in the buying decision?"

**Minutes 22-30: Next Steps**
- "Based on what you've shared, I think we can help with [specific value]. Here's what I recommend as next steps..."
- Schedule technical demo (45 min) with SE
- Identify champion and economic buyer
- Set mutual action plan timeline

# MODULE 7: Customer Success

## Customer Health Score

**Health Score = weighted average of 5 dimensions (0-100)**

| Dimension | Weight | Green (100) | Yellow (50) | Red (0) |
|-----------|--------|-------------|-------------|---------|
| **Usage** (conversations/month vs target) | 30% | ≥80% of target | 50-80% | <50% |
| **Adoption** (active users / total seats) | 20% | ≥70% | 40-70% | <40% |
| **CSAT** (avg satisfaction score) | 20% | ≥4.0 | 3.0-4.0 | <3.0 |
| **Support** (open P1/P2 tickets) | 15% | 0 P1, ≤2 P2 | 1 P1, ≤5 P2 | >1 P1 |
| **Engagement** (last login / last API call) | 15% | <24h | <7 days | >7 days |

**Actions by Health Score:**
- **80-100 (Green)**: Quarterly business review, expansion conversation
- **50-79 (Yellow)**: Weekly check-in, identify blockers, training session
- **0-49 (Red)**: Executive escalation, recovery plan, daily monitoring

## Renewal Process (90 days before renewal)

| Days Before Renewal | Action | Owner |
|---------------------|--------|-------|
| 90 | Review health score + usage trends | CSM |
| 75 | Executive business review with customer | CSM + Account Manager |

| 30 | Send renewal contract | Legal |
| 15 | Follow up (if not signed) | CSM |
| 7 | Escalate to executive sponsor (if still unsigned) | CEO |
