## 1. Distributor Experience Overview

### 1.1 Purpose

The distributor experience helps distributors manage their business effectively across selling, customer support, team growth, learning, and performance tracking.

### 1.2 Experience Goals

- Make distributor work easier and faster.
- Support sales and team growth.
- Improve access to product and business information.
- Make AI assistance useful in daily business activity.
- Help distributors understand progress, incentives, and next actions.

### 1.3 Experience Role

The distributor experience is the operational and growth-oriented business workspace for distributors. It should help them manage their business with clarity, confidence, and momentum.

## 2. Distributor Personas

### 2.1 Persona Catalog

| Persona | Description | Typical Goals | Experience Needs |
|---|---|---|---|
| New Distributor | Recently joined distributor | Learn the business and get started | Strong onboarding, clear guidance, training access |
| Active Distributor | Regular distributor user | Sell products, manage customers, track performance | Fast access, productivity support, AI assistance |
| Team Leader | Distributor managing a team | Support team performance and growth | Team visibility, coaching support, reporting |
| Mobile Distributor | Distributor mainly using mobile channels | Complete tasks quickly from anywhere | Efficient, concise, on-the-go support |
| Growth-Focused Distributor | Distributor focused on expansion | Improve sales and team results | Recommendations, analytics, learning, incentives |

### 2.2 Persona Guidance

- Personas should reflect different maturity levels and responsibilities.
- Each persona should have access to the most relevant business tools and guidance.
- The experience should support both individual success and network growth.

## 3. Distributor Journey

### 3.1 Journey Stages

| Stage | Distributor Need | Experience Goal |
|---|---|---|
| Discovery | Understand the distributor opportunity | Make the business model clear |
| Onboarding | Learn how to start successfully | Reduce uncertainty and accelerate readiness |
| Daily Use | Manage core business activities | Support routine operations efficiently |
| Sales Activity | Recommend and sell products | Improve conversion and customer support |
| Team Growth | Build and support a network | Improve leadership and coaching |
| Learning | Improve skills and knowledge | Support ongoing development |
| Performance Review | Track results and incentives | Improve visibility and motivation |
| Retention | Stay engaged and productive | Reinforce value and success |

### 3.2 Journey Guidance

- The journey should support both new and experienced distributors.
- Daily operations should feel efficient and understandable.
- AI should be present as an assistant to sales, support, and growth.
- The platform should support learning and motivation over time.

## 18. Success Metrics

### 18.1 KPI Catalog

| KPI | Description |
|---|---|
| Distributor Adoption | How many distributors use the platform |
| Task Completion Rate | How often distributors complete key tasks |
| AI Engagement Rate | How often distributors use AI assistance |
| Distributor Satisfaction | How satisfied distributors are with the experience |
| Training Completion Rate | How often distributors complete learning content |
| Incentive Visibility | How clearly distributors understand rewards and progress |
| Productivity Improvement | How much the platform improves distributor work efficiency |

### 18.2 Metric Guidance

- Adoption and task completion show whether the experience is valuable.
- AI engagement should reflect real usefulness.
- Satisfaction should show whether the experience supports confidence.
- Training completion indicates whether the platform supports onboarding and growth.
- Incentive visibility should reflect clarity and motivation.
- Productivity improvement should show the platform helps distributors perform better.

## 19. Experience Governance

### 19.1 Governance Areas

| Governance Area | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Ownership | Distributor experience areas should have clear owners |
| Review | Major journey changes should be reviewed |
| Approval | New or changed experiences should be approved appropriately |
| Consistency | Experiences should remain coherent across distributor touchpoints |
| Documentation | Journeys, standards, and behaviors should be documented |
| Continuous Improvement | Feedback should inform changes |

### 19.2 Guidance

- Ownership should align with distributor business needs.
- Reviews should preserve clarity and usefulness.
- Approval should consider business value and distributor impact.
- Consistency should help distributors move through the platform smoothly.
- Documentation should help teams understand the intended experience.
- Continuous improvement should be informed by distributor feedback and outcomes.
