## 1. Admin Experience Overview

### 1.1 Purpose

The administrator experience supports the people responsible for controlling, governing, configuring, and overseeing the Dayjoy platform and its AI-enabled operations.

### 1.2 Experience Goals

- Make platform management clear and reliable.
- Support safe control of users, content, operations, and AI.
- Improve oversight, accountability, and responsiveness.
- Help administrators act efficiently with confidence.

### 1.3 Experience Role

The admin experience is the control and governance layer for the enterprise platform. It should help administrators maintain order, safety, and consistency across the business.

---

## 2. Administrator Roles

### 2.1 Role Catalog

| Role | Responsibilities | Typical Goals | Experience Needs |
|---|---|---|---|
| System Administrator | Manage system-level settings and platform operations | Keep the platform stable and controlled | Strong oversight, clarity, control |
| AI Administrator | Govern AI behavior, settings, and support quality | Ensure AI remains useful and safe | AI oversight, review, governance |
| Content Administrator | Manage knowledge, guidance, and content quality | Keep content accurate and current | Content control, review, publishing |
| Operations Administrator | Manage business operations and process support | Keep operations efficient and consistent | Operational control, visibility |
| Governance Administrator | Support policy, approvals, and compliance oversight | Ensure adherence to rules and standards | Accountability, auditability |

### 2.2 Role Guidance

- Each role should have a clearly defined scope.
- Administrative experience should reflect privilege and responsibility.
- AI-related administrative responsibilities should be especially well governed.

## 3. Admin Journey

### 3.1 Journey Stages

| Stage | Admin Need | Experience Goal |
|---|---|---|
| Access | Enter the admin environment securely | Make entry clear and controlled |
| Orientation | Understand current priorities and risks | Help admins focus on what matters |
| Management | Update users, settings, and content | Support efficient control actions |
| Oversight | Monitor performance and system conditions | Improve visibility and control |
| Review | Examine logs, approvals, and outcomes | Support accountability |
| Governance | Apply policies and maintain standards | Reinforce controlled operation |
| Improvement | Refine settings and operations over time | Enable continuous platform improvement |

### 3.2 Journey Guidance

- The admin journey should prioritize control, clarity, and accountability.
- Administrative work should be efficient without sacrificing oversight.
- AI should help administrators interpret information and manage complexity.

## 17. Success Metrics

### 17.1 KPI Catalog

| KPI | Description |
|---|---|
| Admin Adoption | How widely administrators use the experience |
| Task Completion Rate | How often admins complete tasks successfully |
| AI Assistance Rate | How often admins use AI support |
| Admin Satisfaction | How satisfied administrators are with the experience |
| Governance Efficiency | How efficiently administrative and governance work is performed |
| Audit Readiness | How ready the platform is for review and oversight |
| Change Confidence | How confident administrators are when making changes |

### 17.2 Metric Guidance

- Adoption and task completion show whether the admin experience is useful.
- AI assistance rate should reflect practical support value.
- Satisfaction should show whether the experience helps administrators feel in control.
- Governance efficiency should reflect reduced friction and stronger oversight.
- Audit readiness should show whether the platform is well controlled.
- Change confidence should reflect trust in administrative actions.
