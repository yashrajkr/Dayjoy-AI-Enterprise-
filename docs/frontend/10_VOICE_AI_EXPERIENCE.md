## 1. Voice AI Experience Overview

### 1.1 Purpose

The Voice AI experience enables users to interact with Dayjoy through spoken conversation to get help, complete tasks, and receive guidance in a hands-free and natural way.

### 1.2 Experience Goals

- Make spoken interaction feel natural and useful.
- Support customers, distributors, employees, and administrators.
- Help users complete tasks without needing to type or navigate extensively.
- Create a confident, respectful, and trustworthy voice experience.

### 1.3 Experience Role

Voice AI is a channel for conversational assistance that should feel fast, accessible, and practical in situations where spoken interaction is preferred or required.

---

## 2. Voice Interaction Principles

| Principle | Description | Why It Matters |
|---|---|---|
| Naturalness | The interaction should feel conversational and easy to use | Reduces friction |
| Clarity | Spoken interaction should be easy to understand | Prevents confusion |
| Brevity | Voice responses should be concise and useful | Supports spoken interaction |
| Confidence | The user should feel the system is dependable | Improves trust |
| Control | Users should feel in charge of the interaction | Reduces frustration |
| Safety | Voice interactions should avoid risky or unclear behavior | Protects users and the business |
| Accessibility | Voice should support users who benefit from hands-free interaction | Increases inclusion |

## 3. User Personas

### 3.1 Persona Catalog

| Persona | Description | Typical Goals | Voice Needs |
|---|---|---|---|
| Customer on the Move | Customer using voice while busy or mobile | Get quick help and answers | Short, clear, helpful voice responses |
| Distributor Using Voice | Distributor using spoken interaction during business activity | Check status, ask questions, complete tasks | Efficient, business-focused voice support |
| Employee Using Voice | Employee using hands-free assistance | Get help with internal tasks and information | Fast, context-aware assistance |
| Administrator Using Voice | Admin using voice for controlled operational help | Request summaries or controlled guidance | Precise, trustworthy, role-aware support |
| Accessibility-Focused User | User relying on voice for accessibility or convenience | Complete tasks without typing | Clear, patient, dependable interaction |

### 3.2 Persona Guidance

- Personas should reflect the user’s environment and urgency.
- Voice interactions should support both quick questions and task-oriented requests.
- The experience should adapt to the user’s role and business context.

## 4. Voice Conversation Lifecycle

### 4.1 Lifecycle Stages

| Stage | User Need | Experience Goal |
|---|---|---|
| Start | Begin speaking naturally | Make it easy to start |
| Listen | Capture the user’s request | Help the user feel heard |
| Understand | Interpret the request | Make the interaction feel intelligent |
| Respond | Provide a spoken answer | Deliver useful information quickly |
| Continue | Support the next exchange | Keep the dialogue coherent |
| Resolve | Help the user finish the task | Make progress visible |
| End | Close the interaction cleanly | Leave the user satisfied |

### 4.2 Lifecycle Guidance

- Voice interactions should begin with low friction.
- The user should understand when the system is listening or responding.
- The interaction should remain concise and task-oriented.
- The conversation should end clearly and respectfully.

## 5. Voice Onboarding

### 5.1 Onboarding Goals

- Help users understand how to talk to the Voice AI.
- Build confidence in spoken interaction.
- Reduce hesitation and uncertainty.

### 5.2 Onboarding Guidance

- Onboarding should explain what kinds of requests work well by voice.
- Users should quickly understand how to start and continue a conversation.
- Voice onboarding should feel simple and practical.

---

## 6. Wake & Activation Experience

### 6.1 Activation Goals

- Help users know how to begin a voice interaction.
- Make activation feel intentional and clear.
- Avoid accidental or confusing starts.

### 6.2 Activation Guidance

- Activation should be easy to understand.
- The user should know when the assistant is ready.
- The interaction should feel respectful of the user’s intent and environment.

---

## 7. Listening Experience

### 7.1 Listening Goals

- Make the user feel heard and understood.
- Reduce repetition and correction.
- Support natural speech patterns.

### 7.2 Listening Guidance

- The experience should tolerate normal human speech variation.
- The user should not feel rushed.
- If something is unclear, the system should recover gracefully.
- Listening should support short and long spoken requests.

---

## 8. Speaking Experience

### 8.1 Speaking Goals

- Make spoken responses easy to follow.
- Keep information concise and practical.
- Support clear next steps.

### 8.2 Speaking Guidance

- Spoken responses should be structured for listening, not reading.
- The assistant should avoid over-explaining unless needed.
- The user should be able to follow the response without effort.
- The experience should support natural conversation flow.

---

## 9. Multi-Turn Voice Conversations

### 9.1 Multi-Turn Goals

- Support more than one exchange naturally.
- Keep the conversation coherent.
- Reduce the need to start over.

### 9.2 Multi-Turn Guidance

- The user should feel that the assistant remembers the current conversation.
- Each turn should build on the previous one.
- The experience should support follow-up questions and refinement.
- The assistant should help the user stay on track.

---

## 10. Voice Confirmation & Clarification

### 10.1 Confirmation Goals

- Ensure the user knows what the assistant understood.
- Reduce mistakes in important or ambiguous requests.
- Build confidence before taking sensitive steps.

### 10.2 Clarification Guidance

- Ask for clarification when the request is unclear.
- Confirm important or high-impact actions before continuing.
- Use simple and direct language for confirmations.
- Keep clarification brief and practical.

---

## 11. Voice Command Experience

### 11.1 Command Goals

- Help users complete structured tasks through speech.
- Make command-driven interaction feel efficient.
- Support business use without forcing complex phrasing.

### 11.2 Command Guidance

- Commands should feel natural and easy to remember.
- The system should support common operational requests.
- The user should know what the command will do.
- The experience should remain simple even for complex work.

---

## 12. Interruptions & Recovery

### 12.1 Recovery Goals

- Help users recover when the conversation is interrupted.
- Preserve confidence and continuity.
- Make it easy to continue after a disruption.

### 12.2 Recovery Guidance

- If the conversation is interrupted, the user should know what to do next.
- The system should help users resume without frustration.
- Recovery should feel calm, not technical.
- The user should not lose confidence because of a disruption.

---

## 13. Human Agent Transfer

### 13.1 Transfer Goals

- Provide a smooth transition when human help is needed.
- Preserve the user’s confidence.
- Keep the experience consistent and supportive.

### 13.2 Transfer Guidance

- Transfer should happen when the task is too sensitive, complex, or unresolved.
- The user should understand why transfer is happening.
- The interaction should remain respectful and reassuring.
- The handoff should not feel like a dead end.

---

## 14. Accessibility Considerations

### 14.1 Accessibility Goals

- Make voice interaction usable for a wide range of users.
- Support hands-free and low-typing use cases.
- Improve inclusion and convenience.

### 14.2 Accessibility Guidance

- Voice should help users who prefer or require speech-based interaction.
- The interaction should be understandable without visual dependence.
- The assistant should support clear pacing and simple structure.
- Accessibility should be a core benefit, not an afterthought.

---

## 15. Privacy & Trust

### 15.1 Trust Goals

- Help users feel safe speaking to the assistant.
- Make privacy expectations clear.
- Build confidence in how the assistant behaves.

### 15.2 Privacy Guidance

- The experience should be respectful of sensitive topics.
- Users should understand when the conversation is private or sensitive.
- The assistant should behave honestly and consistently.
- Trust should be built through clarity, restraint, and reliability.

---

## 16. Performance & Success Metrics

### 16.1 KPI Catalog

| KPI | Description |
|---|---|
| Voice Adoption | How many users use voice interaction |
| Voice Task Completion Rate | How often voice interactions successfully complete tasks |
| Voice AI Engagement Rate | How often users continue using voice AI |
| Voice User Satisfaction | How satisfied users are with the voice experience |
| Clarification Rate | How often the assistant appropriately asks for clarification |
| Transfer Rate | How often users are transferred to a human |
| Voice Efficiency | How quickly users complete tasks through voice |

### 16.2 Metric Guidance

- Adoption should reflect meaningful use, not just availability.
- Task completion shows whether voice is practical for real work.
- Engagement should show that users trust the channel.
- Satisfaction should reflect clarity, convenience, and usefulness.
- Clarification and transfer rates should be interpreted in context.
- Efficiency should show the value of hands-free interaction.

## 17. Voice Experience Governance

### 17.1 Governance Areas

| Governance Area | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Ownership | Voice experience areas should have clear owners |
| Review | Voice journey changes should be reviewed |
| Approval | Significant experience changes should be approved |
| Consistency | Voice should remain coherent across roles and contexts |
| Documentation | Voice behavior standards should be documented |
| Continuous Improvement | Voice feedback should guide changes |

### 17.2 Guidance

- Ownership should align with user experience and channel responsibility.
- Reviews should preserve clarity, trust, and usability.
- Approval should consider user impact and risk.
- Consistency should help users trust the voice experience.
- Documentation should help teams maintain experience quality.
- Continuous improvement should be informed by real usage and feedback.

## 18. Future Voice AI Vision

### 18.1 Future Vision Areas

| Vision Area | Description | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Adaptive Voice Conversations | Voice interactions that adapt more deeply to the user | Future |
| Personalized Voice Experiences | Voice that better reflects user role and preference | Future |
| Multimodal Voice Support | Voice combined with other interaction modes | Future |
| Proactive Spoken Assistance | Voice AI that helps before the user asks | Future |
| Intelligent Voice Workspaces | Voice experiences that support more complete work flows | Future |
| Frictionless Spoken Enterprise Interaction | A smoother and more natural voice experience | Future |

### 18.2 Guidance

- Future voice experience should feel more adaptive and natural.
- Personalization should improve convenience without reducing clarity.
- Multimodal support should make voice more useful in real work.
- Proactive assistance should be helpful without becoming intrusive.
- Intelligent workspaces should make voice a practical enterprise channel.
- Frictionless spoken interaction should improve accessibility and speed.
