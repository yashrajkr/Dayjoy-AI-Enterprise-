## 1. Customer Experience Overview

### 1.1 Purpose

The customer experience defines how customers discover Dayjoy, sign in, explore products, place orders, receive support, and maintain an ongoing relationship with the platform.

### 1.2 Experience Goals

- Make Dayjoy easy to start using.
- Help customers discover products and information quickly.
- Make support and AI assistance easy to access.
- Create confidence in ordering and account management.
- Support long-term engagement and retention.

### 1.3 Experience Role

The customer experience is the primary external-facing journey for Dayjoy’s brand and product relationship. It should make the platform feel useful, trustworthy, and easy to return to.

---

## 2. Customer Personas

### 2.1 Persona Catalog

| Persona | Description | Typical Goals | Experience Needs |
|---|---|---|---|
| New Customer | First-time user exploring Dayjoy | Learn, browse, and make a first purchase | Clear onboarding, product understanding, support access |
| Returning Customer | Existing customer using Dayjoy regularly | Reorder, track orders, get quick help | Fast access, continuity, personalized experience |
| Support-Seeking Customer | Customer with a problem or question | Resolve issue quickly | Clear support paths, knowledge access, AI assistance |
| Product-Focused Customer | Customer comparing products | Find best-fit products | Discovery, comparison, recommendations |
| Mobile-First Customer | Customer primarily using mobile channels | Complete actions quickly on the go | Efficient, accessible, lightweight interactions |

### 2.2 Persona Guidance

- Personas should reflect different levels of experience and urgency.
- Each persona should have a clear path to their most common goals.
- The customer experience should not assume all customers behave the same way.

---

## 3. Customer Journey

### 3.1 Journey Stages

| Stage | Customer Need | Experience Goal |
|---|---|---|
| Discovery | Learn what Dayjoy offers | Make the brand and offer understandable |
| Onboarding | Start using the platform | Reduce friction and build confidence |
| Authentication | Enter the platform securely | Make access smooth and reliable |
| Exploration | Understand products and options | Support discovery and comparison |
| Purchase | Place an order confidently | Make ordering feel clear and secure |
| Tracking | Follow the status of the order | Provide visibility and reassurance |
| Support | Resolve questions or problems | Make help easy to find and use |
| Retention | Keep the customer engaged | Reinforce value and trust |

### 3.2 Journey Guidance

- The journey should feel connected from discovery to repeat use.
- Customers should always understand the next best step.
- The platform should reduce friction at every stage.
- AI should support the journey without making it feel complicated.

## 18. Success Metrics

### 18.1 KPI Catalog

| KPI | Description |
|---|---|
| Customer Adoption | How many customers use the platform |
| Task Completion Rate | How often customers complete desired tasks |
| AI Engagement Rate | How often customers use AI support |
| Customer Satisfaction | How satisfied customers are with the experience |
| Repeat Usage Rate | How often customers return and continue using the platform |
| Support Deflection | How often customers resolve issues without direct support intervention |
| Order Completion Confidence | How confident customers feel in completing orders |

### 18.2 Metric Guidance

- Adoption and repeat usage show whether the platform is valuable.
- Task completion rate shows whether the customer journey works.
- AI engagement rate shows whether AI is useful and discoverable.
- Satisfaction and confidence show whether the experience builds trust.
- Support deflection reflects effective self-service and AI assistance.

## 19. Experience Governance

### 19.1 Governance Areas

| Governance Area | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Ownership | Customer experience areas should have clear owners |
| Review | Major journey changes should be reviewed |
| Approval | New or changed experiences should be approved appropriately |
| Consistency | Customer touchpoints should feel coherent |
| Documentation | Journeys, standards, and behaviors should be documented |
| Continuous Improvement | Feedback should inform changes |

### 19.2 Guidance

- Ownership should align with customer-facing responsibilities.
- Reviews should ensure experiences remain useful and understandable.
- Approval should consider business value and customer impact.
- Consistency across channels should remain a top priority.
- Documentation should keep the customer journey understandable to teams.
- Continuous improvement should be informed by feedback and outcomes.

## 20. Future Customer Experience Vision

### 20.1 Future Vision Areas

| Vision Area | Description | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Adaptive Customer Experiences | Experiences that adapt more intelligently to customer context | Future |
| Personalized Journeys | Journeys that reflect customer preferences and behavior | Future |
| Multimodal Assistance | Customers interact across more than one channel naturally | Future |
| Proactive AI Assistance | AI helps before the customer asks | Future |
| Intelligent Customer Workspaces | Customer-facing spaces that surface what matters most | Future |
| Frictionless Engagement | A more seamless customer journey across touchpoints | Future |

### 20.2 Guidance

- Future customer experience should feel more adaptive and intelligent.
- Personalization should improve relevance without adding complexity.
- Multimodal assistance should make the experience more natural.
- Proactive AI should be helpful and not intrusive.
- Frictionless engagement should improve loyalty and convenience.
