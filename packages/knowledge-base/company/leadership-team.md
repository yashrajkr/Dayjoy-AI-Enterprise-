## 7. Leadership Principles

Although not formally published as a "leadership principles" document, Dayjoy's public communications and the distributor training material suggest the following leadership behaviors:

1. **Lead by example** — Senior leaders are expected to model the company's values in their daily work.
2. **Distributor-first decisions** — When trade-offs arise, decisions should favor distributor and customer interests.
3. **Compliance is non-negotiable** — Leaders are accountable for regulatory compliance in their functions.
4. **Continuous learning** — Leaders are expected to invest in their own development and mentor the next generation.
5. **Transparent communication** — Bad news travels fast; leaders share both good and bad news with their teams promptly.
6. **Data-driven decisions** — Leaders use data, not gut feel, for operational and strategic decisions.
7. **Long-term thinking** — Decisions are made with a multi-year horizon, not quarterly targets.
