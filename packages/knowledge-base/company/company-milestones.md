## 1. Overview

Dayjoy Marketing Private Limited was incorporated on **17 September 2018** in Kota, Rajasthan, India. In the years since, the company has expanded from a founder-led wellness direct selling startup into a multi-brand, multi-state direct selling organization with a network of independent distributors across India.
