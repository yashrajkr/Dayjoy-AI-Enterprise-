## 1. Company Overview

**Dayjoy Marketing Private Limited** is an Indian private limited company and a **wellness brand and direct selling organization** that focuses on providing lifestyle products across health, beauty, wellness, and household categories. The company enables customers to buy products directly from the company or through its network of **independent sales consultants (distributors)**.

Dayjoy describes its purpose as creating **values for customers through products of the highest quality** backed by the guarantee of satisfaction, while maintaining uncompromising values and principles as the company grows. The company positions itself as setting a new benchmark in the direct selling segment in India and creating a world of opportunities so people can live with economic independence.

## 3. Business Model

Dayjoy operates on a **direct selling business model** with the following characteristics:

- **Direct to consumer:** Customers can purchase products directly from the company through the official website (`dayjoy.in`) or via authorized independent distributors.
- **Distributor-led growth:** Independent distributors sell products and earn income through retail profits, performance incentives, and various bonuses.
- **Free distributor registration:** Becoming an Independent Distributor is free, with no joining fee.
- **Multi-brand portfolio:** Dayjoy owns and operates multiple wellness brands across categories.
- **Backward integration:** The company provides complete backward integration to its business family members, resulting in increased business control, better cost control, reduced loss risk, and healthier competitive advantages.

### 3.1 Revenue Streams

1. **Retail sales** — Direct product sales through the website and distributors.
2. **Distributor product purchases** — Distributors buy at Distributor Price (DP) and sell at Maximum Retail Price (MRP), earning up to 30% retail profit.
3. **Repeat purchases** — Wellness and personal care products drive recurring revenue.
