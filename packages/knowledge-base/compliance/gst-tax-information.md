## 4. Input Tax Credit (ITC) for Distributors

### 4.1 ITC Eligibility

Distributors with a valid **GSTIN** can claim input tax credit on:

- Products purchased from Dayjoy for resale (the GST paid on distributor purchases).
- Business expenses (marketing materials, office supplies, vehicles used for business).
- Services used for business (internet, phone, transport).

### 4.2 ITC Claim Process

1. Distributor purchases products from Dayjoy → receives tax invoice with GSTIN.
2. Dayjoy files GSTR-1 → distributor's purchase reflects in their GSTR-2B (auto-populated).
3. Distributor files GSTR-3B → claims ITC based on GSTR-2B.
4. Distributor must match invoice details with GSTR-2B before claiming ITC.

### 4.3 ITC Restrictions

Distributors cannot claim ITC on:

- Purchases for personal consumption (not for business).
- Purchases from unregistered suppliers (without invoice).
- Purchases where invoice is not reflected in GSTR-2B.
- Motor vehicle purchases (above 13-seater) for non-transport use.
- Goods lost, stolen, destroyed, or written off.
- Goods used for personal consumption.

### 4.4 Distributor GST Registration

Distributors must register under GST if their **annual turnover exceeds ₹40 lakh** (for goods; threshold varies by state and product). Below the threshold, registration is optional (composition scheme available below ₹1.5 crore turnover).

## 6. Place of Supply Rules

### 6.1 Sale of Goods

- **Place of supply = Location of goods at time of delivery to recipient.**
- For Dayjoy shipping from Rajasthan:
  - To Rajasthan customer → **CGST + SGST** (intra-state).
  - To other state customer → **IGST** (inter-state).

### 6.2 Distributor Sales to Customers

- When a distributor sells to a customer:
  - If both in same state → CGST + SGST.
  - If in different states → IGST.
- The distributor's invoice determines the tax type, not Dayjoy's invoice to the distributor.

## 11. Customer FAQ on GST

### Q1: Why am I charged GST on my order?

**A:** GST is a mandatory indirect tax levied by the Government of India on the supply of goods and services. Dayjoy is legally required to collect GST on all taxable sales and remit it to the government. The GST amount is included in the MRP of products.

### Q2: Can I get a GST invoice for my business?

**A:** Yes. If you have a valid GSTIN, please provide it at checkout (or update it in your account). The GST invoice will be generated with your GSTIN, and you can use it to claim input tax credit (if eligible).

### Q3: Can I claim ITC on my Dayjoy purchases?

**A:** If you have a valid GSTIN and the purchases are for business purposes (resale or business use), you may be eligible to claim ITC. Personal consumption purchases are not eligible for ITC. Please consult your tax advisor for specific guidance.

### Q4: What if I forgot to add my GSTIN at checkout?

**A:** You can request an updated GST invoice from Customer Care within 30 days of purchase, providing your GSTIN. After 30 days, GST invoice modification is not possible per GST rules.

### Q5: Are the prices displayed on the website inclusive of GST?

**A:** Yes. All prices displayed on `dayjoy.in` are inclusive of GST unless otherwise stated.

### Q6: How is GST calculated on combo packages?

**A:** For combo packages, GST is calculated based on the highest GST rate among the constituent products (as per mixed supply rules under GST). The product page shows the breakup of taxable value and GST.
