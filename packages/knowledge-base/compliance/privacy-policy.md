## 1. Overview

Dayjoy Marketing Private Limited ("Dayjoy," "we," "us," "our") is committed to protecting the privacy of its customers, distributors, website users, and employees. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, share, retain, and protect personal information in compliance with applicable Indian laws, including the **Information Technology Act, 2000**, the **Information Technology (Intermediaries Guidelines) Rules, 2011**, and the **Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP)**.

This policy applies to:

- The official website `dayjoy.in`
- The Dayjoy AI Enterprise Platform (Voice AI, WhatsApp AI, Website Chat, Customer Portal, Distributor Portal, Employee Portal, Admin Dashboard)
- All products and services offered by Dayjoy
- All customers, distributors, employees, and website users

> **VERIFIED:** Dayjoy's Privacy Policy is effective from **12 August 2019**. The policy is published on the official website and references the IT Act, 2000 and IT (Intermediaries Guidelines) Rules, 2011. The Dayjoy AI Enterprise Platform is designed to extend this policy with DPDP Act, 2023 compliance.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

### 3.1 Primary Uses (Consent-Based)

1. **Order fulfillment** — Process orders, ship products, manage returns/refunds.
2. **Account management** — Create and manage your account, authenticate login.
3. **Customer support** — Respond to inquiries, resolve complaints, provide technical support.
4. **Distributor management** — Onboard distributors, calculate commissions, process payouts.
5. **Communication** — Send order updates, promotional offers (with consent), policy updates.
6. **AI-powered services** — Provide personalized AI assistance via Voice, WhatsApp, Web chat.
7. **Compliance** — Meet legal obligations (GST, KYC, consumer protection, DPDP).
8. **Security** — Detect and prevent fraud, unauthorized access, abuse.

### 3.2 Secondary Uses (With Separate Consent)

1. **Marketing and promotions** — Send promotional emails/SMS/WhatsApp (opt-in required).
2. **Product improvement** — Use anonymized usage data to improve products and services.
3. **AI training** — Use conversation data to train and improve AI models (with explicit consent; data is anonymized).
4. **Research and analytics** — Aggregate, anonymized data for business analytics.
5. **Testimonials** — Use your story in marketing materials (with separate signed consent).

### 3.3 Legal Basis for Processing (DPDP Act, 2023)

Under the DPDP Act, 2023, we process personal data based on:

- **Consent** — For marketing, AI training, testimonials (separate consent for each purpose).
- **Contractual necessity** — For order fulfillment, distributor payouts.
- **Legal obligation** — For KYC, GST, tax filings, regulatory compliance.
- **Legitimate interests** — For fraud prevention, security, network integrity.
- **Vital interests** — For product safety recalls, adverse event reporting.

## 6. Data Security

### 6.1 Technical Safeguards

- **Encryption at rest:** All databases and storage encrypted with AWS KMS-managed keys.
- **Encryption in transit:** TLS 1.3 for all data transmission.
- **Access control:** Role-based access control (RBAC) with least-privilege principle.
- **Multi-factor authentication:** Required for all admin and employee access.
- **Network security:** VPC isolation, security groups, WAF, DDoS protection.
- **Audit logging:** Every data access logged and retained for 7 years.
- **Vulnerability management:** Regular penetration testing, SAST/DAST in CI/CD.
- **Data loss prevention:** DLP tools monitor for unauthorized data exfiltration.

### 6.2 Organizational Safeguards

- **Data protection officer (DPO):** [PLACEHOLDER — client to provide DPO contact]
- **Privacy training:** All employees receive annual privacy training.
- **Vendor due diligence:** All service providers undergo security review.
- **Incident response plan:** Documented and tested quarterly.
- **Data processing agreements:** Signed with all service providers.

### 6.3 Data Breach Response

In the event of a personal data breach:

1. **Detection:** Within 24 hours of breach detection.
2. **Containment:** Immediate isolation of affected systems.
3. **Assessment:** Determine scope, severity, affected users.
4. **Notification:** Per DPDP Act, 2023:
   - Notify the **Data Protection Board of India** within 72 hours.
   - Notify **affected users** without unreasonable delay.
5. **Remediation:** Fix vulnerabilities, restore services.
6. **Post-incident review:** Within 30 days; document lessons learned.
