## 1. Overview

This Product Training module equips Dayjoy distributors with deep product knowledge across all 12 categories and 8 brands. Mastery of product knowledge is the foundation of every successful Dayjoy business — without it, distributors cannot make honest recommendations, answer customer questions, or build long-term trust.

### 1.1 Training Objectives

By the end of this training, you will be able to:

1. Explain the benefits, ingredients, and usage of every Dayjoy product you sell.
2. Recommend the right product for a customer's specific need.
3. Handle product-related questions and objections confidently.
4. Demonstrate products safely and compliantly.
5. Recognize when to refer a customer to a healthcare professional.

### 1.2 Compliance First

**CRITICAL:** Every product conversation must follow these compliance rules:

- ❌ **No medical claims.** Never say a product "cures," "treats," "prevents," or "diagnoses" any disease.
- ✅ **Use wellness language.** Say "supports," "may help maintain," "is traditionally used for."
- ✅ **Disclose contraindications.** If a product is not suitable for certain groups (pregnant women, children, specific medical conditions), say so.
- ✅ **Recommend professional consultation.** "Please consult your healthcare professional before use, especially if you have a medical condition or are taking medication."
- ❌ **No exaggerated claims.** Don't promise "miracle results," "100% success," or "overnight transformation."
- ✅ **Disclose average results.** "Individual results vary based on usage, lifestyle, and consistency."

- **Toothpaste:** Squeeze a small amount on a brush; describe the taste and texture.

### 2.3 Germanium & Magnetic

#### 2.3.1 Category Overview

- **Focus:** Wellness jewelry with embedded magnets or germanium.
- **Sub-categories:** Magnetic bracelets, necklaces, belts, insoles.
- **Target customer:** Adults aged 35–70, especially those with active lifestyles or joint comfort interests.
- **Differentiator:** Fashionable designs combined with wellness orientation.

#### 2.3.2 Compliance Note (Critical)

Germanium & Magnetic products are **wellness accessories**, NOT medical devices. Distributors must NOT claim:

- ❌ "Cures arthritis"
- ❌ "Treats joint pain"
- ❌ "Improves blood circulation" (without substantiation)
- ❌ "Relieves inflammation"

Distributors MAY say:

- ✅ "Traditional wellness accessory"
- ✅ "Worn for comfort and style"
- ✅ "Many users report a sense of well-being"
- ✅ "Popular in wellness-focused communities"

#### 2.3.3 Contraindications

- People with pacemakers should consult their doctor before using magnetic products.
- Pregnant women should consult their doctor.
- Not suitable for children under 12.

### 2.4 Color and Cosmetics

#### 2.4.1 Category Overview

- **Focus:** Affordable, Indian-skin-tone-friendly cosmetics.
- **Sub-categories:** Lipstick, foundation, kajal, nail polish, compact, blush.
- **Differentiator:** Formulated for Indian skin tones; affordable pricing; long-lasting.

#### 2.4.2 Demonstration Tips

- Always patch-test on the back of the customer's hand before applying to the face.
- Use disposable applicators for hygiene.
- Show the shade range; help the customer pick a shade for their skin tone.
- Demonstrate staying power on the hand.

#### 2.4.3 Allergy Precautions

- Ask about cosmetic allergies before application.
- Discontinue use if irritation occurs.
- For sensitive skin, recommend a 24-hour patch test on the inner wrist.

## 3. Product Demonstration Framework

### 3.1 The 5-Step Demonstration

1. **Introduce** — "I'd like to show you [Product Name] from Dayjoy's [Category] range."
2. **Highlight key benefit** — "This product is designed to [key benefit]."
3. **Show ingredients** — "It contains [key ingredients], which are known for [traditional use / general benefit]."
4. **Demonstrate usage** — "Here's how you use it..." [demonstrate].
5. **Address compliance** — "Please note: this is a wellness product, not a medicine. Consult your healthcare professional before use, especially if you have a medical condition."

### 3.2 Safety Precautions

- Always patch-test cosmetics and skin care products.
- Always check for allergies before application.
- Use disposable applicators for cosmetics.
- Keep products away from children.
- Stop use immediately if irritation occurs.
- For internal-use products (Health Care, Food), always mention dosage and contraindications.
