## 1. Overview

Welcome to Dayjoy! This onboarding training is designed to take you from a new Independent Distributor to a confident, productive business builder within **30 days**. The training is structured into 5 modules, each focusing on a critical aspect of your Dayjoy business.

### 1.1 Onboarding Goals

By the end of this onboarding, you will be able to:

1. Understand Dayjoy's mission, vision, and product portfolio.
2. Explain the compensation plan and how you earn income.
3. Demonstrate product knowledge for your first 5 sales.
4. Recruit and onboard your first distributor (within 30 days).
5. Use the Distributor Portal and AI tools to manage your business.

### 1.2 Time Commitment

| Module | Duration | Mode |
|---|---|---|
| Module 1: Company & Brand | 2 hours | Self-paced (portal) |
| Module 2: Products | 3 hours | Self-paced (portal) + product samples |
| Module 3: Compensation Plan | 2 hours | Self-paced + live Q&A |
| Module 4: Sales & Recruiting | 3 hours | Live workshop (online or in-person) |

| **Total** | **12 hours** (over 30 days) | Hybrid |

## 5. Module 4 — Sales & Recruiting

### 5.1 Learning Objectives

- Conduct a successful product sale from lead to delivery.
- Identify and approach qualified distributor prospects.
- Handle common objections effectively.
- Onboard a new distributor successfully.

### 5.2 Key Content

#### 5.2.1 The 7-Step Sales Process

1. **Prospecting** — Identify potential customers (warm market: friends, family; cold market: events, social media).
2. **Approach** — Initial contact; build rapport; identify needs.
3. **Presentation** — Show product benefits tied to customer needs; demonstrate.
4. **Trial close** — "How does this sound?" or "Would you like to try it?"
5. **Objection handling** — Address concerns (see objection handling section).
6. **Close** — Confirm sale; collect payment; arrange delivery.
7. **Follow-up** — Post-sale check-in; request referral; pitch business opportunity.

#### 5.2.2 Common Objections & Responses

| Objection | Response Strategy |
|---|---|
| "It's too expensive" | Show value per use; compare with alternatives; offer combo discount. |
| "I've never heard of this brand" | Share Dayjoy's history (2018, Kota, 9 states); show testimonials. |
| "Does it really work?" | Share personal experience; show testimonials; offer satisfaction guarantee. |
| "I need to think about it" | Ask what specifically they want to think about; address each concern. |
| "I'm already using another brand" | Acknowledge; offer to compare; suggest trying one product first. |
| "Is this a pyramid scheme?" | Explain legitimate direct selling: free onboarding, real products, buyback policy, retail-focused compensation. |

#### 5.2.3 Recruiting Process

1. **Identify prospects** — Entrepreneurs, part-time income seekers, stay-at-home parents, students, retirees.
2. **Approach** — Don't pitch on first contact. Build relationship; share your story.
3. **Invite to learn more** — "I'd love to share what I'm working on. Can we chat for 15 minutes?"
4. **Present opportunity** — Use the official Dayjoy presentation; emphasize free onboarding, multiple income streams, training.
5. **Address concerns** — Especially pyramid scheme concern (Section 5.2.2).
6. **Close** — Help them register online; provide welcome kit.
7. **Onboard** — Walk them through this Module 1-5 training.

#### 5.2.4 Compliance Reminder

When recruiting, you must:

- Disclose your name, distributor ID, contact.
- Provide the official Dayjoy business plan document.
- Disclose average earnings (per Income Disclosure Statement).
- Disclose that income depends on effort and results vary.
- Disclose the buyback policy.
- Provide Grievance Officer contact.
- Disclose no mandatory product purchase to join.
- Provide cooling-off period information.

### 5.3 Activity

- Practice objection handling with your upline (role-play 5 scenarios).
- Make your first 5 product sales within 14 days of completing Module 4.
- Recruit your first distributor within 30 days of completing Module 4.

## 6. Module 5 — Tools & Operations

### 6.1 Learning Objectives

- Use the Distributor Portal effectively.
- Use the AI tools (WhatsApp AI, Voice AI) for business support.
- Manage orders, commissions, and downline.
- Stay compliant with all policies.

### 6.2 Key Content

#### 6.2.1 Distributor Portal (Phase 2)

The Distributor Portal (URL: [PLACEHOLDER — `portal.dayjoy.in/distributor`]) provides:

- **Dashboard** — Your sales, commissions, rank progress, downline activity.
- **Catalog** — Browse products with DP, MRP, BV, PV.
- **Order placement** — Place orders for yourself or customers.
- **Downline tree** — View your binary tree, ranks, activity.
- **Commissions** — View earnings, payout history, download statements.
- **Training** — Access training modules, recorded webinars.
- **Marketing kit** — Download approved product images, brochures, captions.
- **Support** — Raise tickets, chat with AI assistant, contact Business Support.

#### 6.2.2 AI Assistant Usage

You can use the Dayjoy AI assistants for:

- **Product questions** — "What are the benefits of Asthprash?"
- **Compensation questions** — "How do I qualify for Silver Executive?"
- **Policy questions** — "What is the return policy?"
- **Order status** — "Where is my order?"
- **Training help** — "How do I handle the 'too expensive' objection?"

AI assistants are available 24/7 via WhatsApp, Voice (call the Business Support line), and Web Chat (on the Distributor Portal).

#### 6.2.3 Order Management

- **Personal orders** — For your own use or demonstration samples.
- **Customer orders** — Placed on behalf of customers (with their delivery address).
- **Subscription orders** — Set up recurring orders (e.g., monthly wellness combo).
- **Bulk orders** — For events, training sessions, gifting.

#### 6.2.4 Commission Management

- Commissions are calculated weekly (BV-based) and monthly (rank-based).
- Payouts processed to your registered bank account.
- TDS deducted at 10% if annual commission exceeds ₹15,000 (Section 194H).
- Commission statements available in the portal; Form 16A issued quarterly.
- Always reconcile your commission statement with your records.

#### 6.2.5 Compliance Checklist (Daily)

- ✅ Made only verified product claims (no medical claims).
- ✅ Disclosed average earnings when recruiting.
- ✅ Did not sell on unauthorized marketplaces (Amazon, Flipkart).
- ✅ Did not make exaggerated income promises.
- ✅ Did not target minors for recruitment.
- ✅ Did not engage in cross-recruitment.
- ✅ Used only approved marketing materials.
