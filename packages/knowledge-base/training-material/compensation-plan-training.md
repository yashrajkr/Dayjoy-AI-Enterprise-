## 1. Overview

This Compensation Plan Training module helps Dayjoy distributors fully understand the income opportunity, calculate their earnings accurately, explain the plan to prospects without exaggeration, and build a strategy for rank advancement. Mastery of the compensation plan is essential for both maximizing your own income and ethically recruiting new distributors.

### 1.1 Training Objectives

By the end of this training, you will be able to:

1. Explain every income stream in the Dayjoy compensation plan.
2. Calculate retail profit, performance incentive, and bonus income accurately.
3. Understand rank advancement criteria for each rank.
4. Explain the compensation plan to a new prospect in under 10 minutes.
5. Build a 90-day plan to advance to your next rank.

### 1.2 Compliance First

**CRITICAL — Income Disclosure Requirements:**

- Always reference the Income Disclosure Statement (IDS) when discussing income.
- Use the official language: "In 2025, the average annual income for an active Dayjoy distributor was ₹[X]. See dayjoy.in/income-disclosure. Results not typical."
- Never promise specific income amounts.
- Never imply that recruitment is the primary income source — retail sales is the foundation.
- Always disclose that income depends on effort, skill, and market conditions.

> **[PLACEHOLDER — IMPORTANT]:** The Income Disclosure Statement is not yet published. This is a critical compliance gap. Until it's published, distributors should not quote specific income figures and should refer prospects to the official IDS when available.

## 2. Dayjoy Compensation Plan — Overview

### 2.1 The Foundation: Retail Sales

The Dayjoy compensation plan is built on **retail sales** — distributors earning the difference between Distributor Price (DP) and Maximum Retail Price (MRP).

- **MRP** — Maximum Retail Price printed on the product.
- **DP** — Distributor Price at which distributors buy from Dayjoy.
- **Retail Profit** — MRP minus DP, which can be up to **30%** of MRP.

**Example:**
- A product with MRP ₹1,000 has DP approximately ₹700.
- Distributor buys at ₹700, sells at ₹1,000.
- Retail profit = ₹300 (30% of MRP).

### 2.3 Key Concepts

#### Business Volume (BV)

- BV is the **point value** assigned to each product.
- BV is used to calculate most incentives (Performance Incentive, Binary, etc.).
- BV is typically a percentage of the DP (e.g., a product with DP ₹1,000 may have BV of 800).
- BV accumulates from your personal sales + your downline's sales.

#### Point Value (PV)

- PV is used for **rank advancement** calculations.
- PV is generated by your personal sales and team sales.
- Different ranks require different PV thresholds.

#### Binary Structure

- Dayjoy uses a **binary compensation structure** for the Business Matching Incentive.
- Each distributor has **2 legs** (Left and Right).
- New recruits are placed in either the Left or Right leg.
- Business Matching Incentive is paid based on the **matching BV** between the two legs.

## 3. Income Streams — Detailed Explanation

### 3.1 Retail Profit (Income Stream 1)

- **What it is:** The profit you earn by selling products at MRP that you bought at DP.
- **Who earns it:** Every distributor, from day one.
- **How it's calculated:** MRP − DP = Retail Profit (up to 30% of MRP).
- **When it's paid:** Immediately (when you sell and collect payment).
- **Example:**
  - You sell ₹10,000 worth of products in a month.
  - Retail profit at 30% = ₹3,000/month.

## 7. Common Compensation Plan Questions

### Q1: When are commissions paid?

**A:** Commission payout schedule:
- Retail profit: Immediately (you keep the difference when you sell).
- Performance Incentive, Active Bonus, Leadership Bonus: Monthly (by [PLACEHOLDER] of next month).
- Business Matching Incentive: Weekly (by [PLACEHOLDER] of next week).
- Rank advancement bonuses: Within 30 days of rank achievement.
- Travel/Rewards: Per published schedule.

### Q2: How is TDS handled on commissions?

**A:** Per Section 194H of the Income Tax Act, Dayjoy deducts TDS at **10%** on commission income if your annual commission exceeds ₹15,000. TDS certificate (Form 16A) is issued quarterly. You can claim TDS credit when filing your income tax return.

### Q3: What happens to my commissions if I miss monthly maintenance?

**A:** If you miss monthly maintenance:
- You may not qualify for Active Bonus and Performance Incentive for that month.
- Your rank is retained during the grace period ([PLACEHOLDER — typical: 1 month]).
- After grace period, rank is downgraded.
- Previously earned commissions are not clawed back.

### Q4: What happens if my customer returns a product?

**A:** If a customer returns a product within the return window:
- The BV/PV from that sale is reversed.
- Any commission already paid on that BV is adjusted in the next payout.
- Your rank maintenance calculation is updated to reflect the reversal.

### Q5: Can I lose my rank?

**A:** Yes, if you fail to meet monthly maintenance criteria:
- After [PLACEHOLDER — typical: 1 month] of non-maintenance, you are placed in "Inactive" status.
- After [PLACEHOLDER — typical: 3 months] of non-maintenance, your rank is downgraded one level.
- You can re-qualify for the higher rank by meeting criteria again.

### Q6: What happens to my downline if I leave Dayjoy?

**A:** If you choose to exit Dayjoy:
- Your downline is reassigned per the Business Plan (typically to your upline).
- Your unpaid commissions are settled within 30 days.
- You are eligible for buyback of unsold inventory at ≥90% of price.
- You forfeit all future commission rights from your former downline.

### Q7: Is there a cap on earnings?

**A:** Yes, the Business Matching Incentive has a **daily/weekly cap** based on your rank. Higher ranks have higher caps. There is no cap on retail profit, Performance Incentive, or Leadership Bonus (subject to plan terms).

### Q8: Can I see my commissions in real-time?

**A:** Yes, the Distributor Portal (Phase 2) shows:
- Pending commissions (not yet paid).
- Approved commissions (to be paid next cycle).
- Paid commissions (with bank reference).
- BV/PV accumulation in real-time.

## 8. Explaining the Plan to Prospects

### 8.1 The 10-Minute Compensation Plan Pitch

When explaining the plan to a prospect, keep it under 10 minutes:

**Minute 1: Foundation (Retail Profit)**
> "Dayjoy's foundation is retail profit. You buy at Distributor Price, sell at MRP, and keep the difference — up to 30%."

**Minute 2-3: The Opportunity (Team BV)**
> "Beyond retail, you can build a team. When your team sells, you earn based on the Business Volume they generate. The more active your team, the more you earn."

**Minute 4-5: Binary Income**
> "Dayjoy uses a binary structure — you have a Left leg and a Right leg. You earn matching bonuses based on the BV in both legs. This is paid weekly."

**Minute 6-7: Ranks and Bonuses**
> "As you grow, you advance through 7 ranks — from Independent Distributor to Emerald Vice President. Each rank unlocks new bonuses: Leadership, Mentorship, Travel, Car, House, Lifestyle funds."

**Minute 8: Free to Start**
> "Best of all — joining is **free**. No joining fee, no mandatory inventory purchase. You can start today."

**Minute 9: Income Disclosure**
> "I want to be transparent: in 2025, the average annual income for an active Dayjoy distributor was ₹[X]. Results vary based on effort. See dayjoy.in/income-disclosure for full details."

**Minute 10: Next Steps**
> "Would you like to register and try it out? I'll walk you through the onboarding."

### 8.2 What NOT to Say

- ❌ "You can earn ₹1 lakh per month easily."
- ❌ "Just recruit 10 people and you'll be rich."
- ❌ "This is guaranteed income."
- ❌ "Pyramid structure pays exponentially."
- ❌ "Average distributors earn ₹50,000+/month." (unless true per IDS)

### 8.3 What TO Say

- ✅ "Your income depends on your effort."
- ✅ "It takes 6–12 months of consistent effort to see meaningful income."
- ✅ "The foundation is product sales — not recruitment."
- ✅ "Joining is free, and you can exit anytime with buyback."
- ✅ "Here's the official Income Disclosure Statement showing average earnings."
