Some remote PIN codes may not be serviceable by Dayjoy's logistics partners. In such cases:

3. If no alternate address is available, full refund is processed within 3–5 business days.

## 5. Order Tracking

### 5.1 Tracking Number

- A tracking number is shared with the customer via SMS, email, and WhatsApp within 24 hours of dispatch.
- The customer can track their order on:
  - The Dayjoy website (Order Tracking page) [PLACEHOLDER — confirm URL]

### 5.2 Tracking Status Updates

Customers receive SMS/WhatsApp/email updates at each stage:

1. Order placed (confirmation)
2. Order processed (packed)
3. Order dispatched (with tracking number)
4. In transit (with location updates from courier)
5. Out for delivery (on the delivery day)
6. Delivered (with delivery confirmation)
7. Delivery failed (if applicable, with reason and next attempt)

The Dayjoy AI Enterprise Platform (Phase 2) will enable customers to:

## 6. Delivery & Handoff

### 6.1 Delivery Attempts

- **Standard:** Up to 3 delivery attempts by the courier.
- **Gap between attempts:** 1–2 business days.
- **Failed delivery after 3 attempts:** Order is returned to Dayjoy warehouse; refund is processed within 7–10 business days.

### 6.2 Delivery Confirmation

- Courier obtains a signature or OTP-based delivery confirmation.
- Photo proof of delivery may be captured for high-value orders.
- Customer receives delivery confirmation via SMS/email.

### 6.3 Open Box Delivery

For high-value or fragile items, Dayjoy offers open-box delivery (where the courier waits while the customer opens and inspects the package). To request:

- Delivery timeline may extend to 7–10 business days.
- [PLACEHOLDER — confirm which products are classified as heavy/bulky]

### 8.3 Combo / Bundle Orders

- Combo packages are shipped together in a single consignment.
- If any item in the combo is out of stock, the entire combo is held until all items are available (or customer is offered a partial shipment with the balance to follow).

### 8.4 Festival / Peak Season Shipping

- During peak seasons (Diwali, Raksha Bandhan, end-of-year), delivery timelines may extend by 2–3 business days.
- Dayjoy communicates peak season cutoff dates for guaranteed delivery via website banners and email campaigns.

## 9. Shipping Delays

### 9.1 Common Causes of Delay

- Weather disruptions (monsoons, cyclones, fog).
- Festival / holiday peaks overwhelming courier capacity.
- Address inaccuracies or incomplete PIN codes.
- Customer unavailability for delivery.
- Customs / state border checks (rare).
- Force majeure events (strikes, natural disasters, pandemics).

### 9.2 Customer Actions on Delay

If an order has not been delivered within the standard timeline + 5 business days:

1. Track the order using the tracking number.
2. If tracking shows no update for 48 hours, contact Customer Care.
3. Customer Care raises a ticket with the courier partner.
4. If the courier cannot resolve within 5 business days, Dayjoy offers refund or replacement.

### 9.3 Dayjoy's Commitment

- Transparent communication about delays.
- Proactive notifications for known disruptions.
- No cancellation fees for orders delayed beyond [PLACEHOLDER — typical: 15 business days] due to Dayjoy's fault.
