## 1. Policy Overview

Dayjoy Marketing Private Limited offers a return and refund policy designed to protect customer rights while maintaining fair business practices. This policy applies to all customers purchasing products through the official website (`dayjoy.in`), authorized distributors, franchisee outlets, or the company office.

### 1.1 Policy Applicability

| Stakeholder | Applicable |
|---|---|
| Customer purchasing for personal use | ✅ Yes |
| Independent Distributor (for personal use purchases) | ✅ Yes |
| Independent Distributor (for resale stock) | ⚠️ Subject to distributor-specific terms |
| Website users | ✅ Yes |
| Bulk / institutional buyers | ⚠️ Subject to separate contract terms |

### 1.2 Governing Regulations

- **Consumer Protection Act, 2019** (India)
- **Direct Selling Guidelines** issued by Government of India (9 September 2016)
- **Information Technology Act, 2000** and IT (Intermediaries Guidelines) Rules, 2011

### 2.2 Conditions for Return

A product is eligible for return only if ALL of the following conditions are met:

1. The return request is initiated within the applicable return window.
2. The product is unused, undamaged, and in its original packaging (for change-of-mind returns).
3. For defective/damaged products: the customer provides photos/videos as evidence.
4. The original invoice, packaging, and all accessories are returned with the product.
5. The product is not in the list of non-returnable items (see Section 2.4).

### 2.3 Return Initiation Process

1. **Contact Customer Care** within the return window via phone, email, or WhatsApp.
2. **Provide order details:** Order ID, product name, reason for return, photos (if damaged/defective).
3. **Receive Return Authorization:** Customer Care issues a Return Authorization Number (RAN) and return shipping instructions.
4. **Ship the product:** Customer ships the product to the address provided (or schedules a pickup if available).
5. **Inspection:** Dayjoy inspects the returned product within [PLACEHOLDER — typical: 3–5 business days].
6. **Resolution:** Refund or replacement processed within [PLACEHOLDER — typical: 7 business days] of inspection approval.

### 2.4 Non-Returnable Items

The following items are typically non-returnable:

- Products that have been opened or used (unless defective).
- Products with broken seals or tampered packaging.
- Perishable items (food products, certain personal care items).
- Products purchased during clearance / final sale promotions.
- Combo / bundle packages where one item has been used.
- Products returned without original packaging and accessories.

> **Note:** Exact refund processing times are not explicitly stated in public sources. [PLACEHOLDER — client to confirm]

### 3.2 Refund Calculation

| Scenario | Refund Amount |
|---|---|
| Defective / damaged product (company fault) | 100% refund + return shipping reimbursement |
| Wrong product shipped (company fault) | 100% refund + return shipping reimbursement |
| Product not as described (company fault) | 100% refund + return shipping reimbursement |
| Change of mind (customer fault) | 100% product refund; return shipping borne by customer |
| Partial use / partial set return | Prorated refund at Dayjoy's discretion |

### 3.3 Refund Exclusions

- Original shipping charges (non-refundable for change-of-mind returns).
- COD convenience fees (non-refundable).
- Promotional / discount amounts already applied (refunds calculated on actual paid amount, not MRP).
- Bank / payment gateway charges (borne by customer for change-of-mind returns).

## 4. Replacement Policy

If a customer prefers replacement over refund (and a replacement is available):

1. Customer must request replacement at the time of return initiation.
2. Replacement is subject to product availability.
3. If replacement is unavailable, refund is processed automatically.
4. Replacement is shipped within [PLACEHOLDER — typical: 5–7 business days] of inspection approval.
5. Return shipping for replacement is borne by Dayjoy (for defective/damaged/wrong product cases).

## 5. Cancellation Policy

### 5.1 Order Cancellation Before Shipping

- **Cancellation window:** Until the order is dispatched (typically within [PLACEHOLDER — typical: 24 hours] of order placement).
- **Refund:** 100% refund processed within [PLACEHOLDER — typical: 3–5 business days].
- **How to cancel:** Contact Customer Care via phone, email, WhatsApp, or self-service through the Customer Portal (Phase 2).

### 5.2 Order Cancellation After Shipping

- Order cannot be cancelled once shipped.
- Customer may refuse delivery; the order will be returned to Dayjoy.
- Refund processed after return inspection (within 7–10 business days of return receipt).
- Return shipping charges may apply.

### 5.3 Distributor Order Cancellation

- Subject to distributor-specific terms in the Terms and Conditions.
- Buyback policy per Direct Selling Guidelines: Dayjoy offers a buyback policy for distributors who choose to exit the business, repurchasing unsold inventory at not less than 90% of the original price. [PLACEHOLDER — client to confirm buyback terms]

## 6. Exchange Policy

Customers may request an exchange in lieu of a refund for:

- Wrong size / variant (for applicable products like clothing).
- Wrong shade (for cosmetics).
- Different product of equal or lesser value (with refund of difference).

Exchange process mirrors the return process (Section 2.3) — customer must initiate within the return window, return the original product, and the new product is shipped upon inspection approval.

## 7. Special Cases

### 7.1 Missing Products / Short Shipment

If a customer receives a package with missing items:

1. Contact Customer Care within [PLACEHOLDER — typical: 48 hours] of delivery.
2. Provide order ID, list of missing items, and photos of received package.
3. Dayjoy verifies with dispatch records and CCTV footage at the warehouse.
4. Missing items are shipped at no additional cost, or refund is processed.

### 7.2 Damaged in Transit

If the product is damaged during shipping:

1. Customer should refuse delivery (if visible damage) OR
2. Accept delivery and contact Customer Care within [PLACEHOLDER — typical: 48 hours] with photos.
3. Return shipping is borne by Dayjoy.
4. Replacement or refund processed.

### 7.3 Product Recall

In case of a product recall (rare):

1. Dayjoy notifies all affected customers via email, SMS, and website notice.
2. Customers are instructed to stop using the product immediately.
3. Free replacement or full refund is offered.
4. Return shipping is borne by Dayjoy.
