## 1. Policy Overview

Dayjoy Marketing Private Limited offers product warranties on selected products in its catalog — primarily on durable goods in the **Germanium & Magnetic**, **Aqua Essentials**, and certain **Home Care** categories. Wellness, personal care, cosmetics, food products, and consumables are not warrantied due to their nature, but are covered by the standard **Return & Refund Policy** for damaged/defective items.

> **CAUTION:** Dayjoy's official warranty terms are not comprehensively documented in reviewed public sources. The terms below are typical for direct selling companies in India. Items marked `[PLACEHOLDER]` require client confirmation before being used in customer-facing AI responses.

### 1.1 What is Covered

This warranty covers **manufacturing defects** in materials and workmanship for the products listed in Section 2. It does not cover damage caused by misuse, neglect, accident, or unauthorized modification.

### 1.2 Warranty Period

Warranty periods vary by product category. See Section 2 for specific periods.

## 3. Warranty Conditions

### 3.1 What the Warranty Covers

- Manufacturing defects in materials
- Manufacturing defects in workmanship
- Product failure under normal use conditions
- Premature wear under normal use (within warranty period)

### 3.2 What the Warranty Does NOT Cover

- Damage from misuse, abuse, neglect, or accident
- Damage from unauthorized repair or modification
- Damage from improper storage or handling
- Normal wear and tear (e.g., fading of cosmetic finishes)
- Damage from use beyond product specifications
- Cosmetic damage (scratches, dents) not affecting functionality
- Damage from natural disasters (fire, flood, lightning)
- Products purchased from unauthorized sellers / non-Distributor channels
- Products with altered or removed serial numbers
- Products without original invoice / proof of purchase
- Products used for commercial purposes (where residential warranty applies)

## 4. Warranty Claim Process

### 4.1 Step-by-Step Process

1. **Initiate claim:** Contact Customer Care via phone, email, or WhatsApp within the warranty period.
2. **Provide details:**
   - Order ID or invoice number
   - Product name and model
   - Serial number (if applicable)
   - Date of purchase
   - Description of defect
   - Photos/videos of the defect
3. **Receive Claim Reference Number (CRN):** Customer Care issues a CRN within [PLACEHOLDER — typical: 24 hours].
4. **Diagnosis:** Dayjoy's technical team reviews the claim and may:
   - Approve the claim immediately (clear defect).
   - Request additional photos/videos.
   - Request the product be sent for physical inspection.
   - Dispatch a technician for on-site inspection (for large items like water purifiers).
5. **Resolution:** Within [PLACEHOLDER — typical: 7 business days] of claim approval:
   - Free repair (preferred where possible)
   - Free replacement (if repair is not feasible)
   - Refund (if neither repair nor replacement is feasible)
6. **Closure:** Customer confirms resolution; CRN is closed.

### 4.2 On-Site Service (for Large Items)

For water purifiers and other large appliances:

- Dayjoy dispatches a technician to the customer's address.
- Service is free within warranty period (for covered defects).
- Technician visit fee may apply for non-covered issues (e.g., customer-cause damage, installation issues).
- On-site service is available in serviceable PIN codes only.

### 4.3 Carry-In Service (for Small Items)

For small items (magnetic bracelets, accessories):

- Customer ships the product to the Dayjoy service center.
- Return shipping after repair/replacement is borne by Dayjoy (for valid claims).
- Forward shipping to service center is borne by customer for change-of-mind claims; by Dayjoy for valid warranty claims.

## 6. Warranty Transferability

- **Standard warranty:** Transferable to a new owner within the warranty period, provided the original invoice is transferred.
- **Extended warranty:** Non-transferable; applies only to the original purchaser.
- **Distributor warranty:** If a distributor sells a product to a customer, the warranty applies to the customer from the original invoice date (distributor's purchase date).

## 7. Out-of-Warranty Repairs

For products outside the warranty period:

- Dayjoy offers paid repair services for selected product categories.
- Customer obtains a repair quote from Customer Care.
- If customer approves, product is sent for repair.
- Repair cost includes parts + labor + return shipping.
- Repair warranty: 90 days on the repaired part only.

---

## 8. Refurbished / Replacement Units

If a product is replaced under warranty, the replacement unit may be:

- A new unit (preferred where available).
- A refurbished unit (for older / discontinued models).
- Refurbished units carry the balance of the original warranty or a 90-day warranty, whichever is longer.

## 10. Customer Responsibilities

To keep the warranty valid, the customer must:

1. Use the product as intended per the user manual.
2. Use only Dayjoy-authorized parts and accessories (where applicable).
3. Perform recommended maintenance (e.g., filter replacement for water purifiers).
4. Retain the original invoice and warranty card.
5. Notify Dayjoy of any defect within a reasonable time (within 30 days of discovery).
6. Allow Dayjoy or its authorized technician to inspect the product.
