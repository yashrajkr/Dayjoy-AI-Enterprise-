## 2. Order & Delivery Issues

### 2.1 Issue: Order Not Delivered Within Estimated Time

**Symptoms:**
- Customer has not received the order beyond the standard delivery timeline + 5 business days.
- Tracking number shows no update for 48+ hours.

**Common Causes:**
- Courier delay (weather, festival peak, logistics issue).
- Incorrect or incomplete delivery address.
- Customer unavailable for delivery.
- Order stuck at state-border check (rare).
- Tracking number not yet scanned by courier.

**Troubleshooting Steps:**
1. Verify the order ID and tracking number with the customer.
2. Check the courier tracking page (link shared via SMS/email/WhatsApp at dispatch).
3. If tracking shows "in transit" with recent update: reassure customer, ask to wait 2–3 more business days.
4. If tracking shows no update for 48+ hours:
   - Raise a ticket with the courier partner (Customer Care).
   - Inform customer of expected resolution: 3–5 business days.
5. If tracking shows "delivered" but customer denies receipt:
   - Request photo proof of delivery from courier.
   - Verify delivery address with customer.
   - Check with neighbors / family members.
   - If genuinely not delivered, process replacement or refund within 7 business days.
6. If tracking shows "returned to sender":
   - Inform customer; ask if address was correct.
   - Offer re-dispatch with corrected address (additional shipping fee may apply) or refund.

**Escalate to Human Agent When:**
- Order not delivered 15+ business days after dispatch.
- Courier confirms loss of package.
- Customer reports theft / misdelivery.

### 2.2 Issue: Received Wrong Product

**Symptoms:**
- Customer received a product different from what was ordered.
- Product name, size, color, or variant does not match order.

**Troubleshooting Steps:**
1. Verify the order details (what was ordered vs. what was received).
2. Ask customer to share photos of: received product, packaging, invoice.
3. Confirm it is a Dayjoy dispatch error (not customer ordering confusion).
4. Initiate return under "Wrong Product Received" — return shipping borne by Dayjoy.
5. Ship correct product within 2–3 business days of return pickup.
6. If correct product is out of stock: offer alternative, store credit, or full refund.

**Escalate When:**
- Pattern of wrong-product errors from same warehouse (quality control issue).
- Customer dissatisfied with resolution.

---

### 2.3 Issue: Missing Items in Order

**Symptoms:**
- Customer received the package but one or more items are missing.

**Troubleshooting Steps:**
1. Ask customer to retain all packaging and not discard anything.
2. Request photos of: received package (with contents), packing slip, packaging condition.
3. Verify with dispatch records (weight check, CCTV at warehouse).
4. If missing items confirmed: ship missing items within 2–3 business days at no cost.
5. If weight check suggests items were packed (rare theft / lost in transit): investigate with courier.
6. Provide customer with timeline: 3–5 business days for verification + 2–3 business days for shipping.

**Escalate When:**
- Verification cannot confirm dispatch discrepancy.
- Customer disputes verification outcome.

---

### 2.4 Issue: Order Cancellation Not Processed

**Symptoms:**
- Customer cancelled order before shipping, but money was debited.
- Order shows as "shipped" despite cancellation request.

**Troubleshooting Steps:**
1. Check order status in system — if still "processing", cancel immediately and process refund within 3–5 business days.
2. If order is "shipped":
   - Inform customer that cancellation is not possible post-dispatch.
   - Offer option to refuse delivery — order will be returned and refund processed (7–10 business days after return receipt).
3. If customer already received the order, treat as a return (see `support/return-policy.md`).

## 3. Payment Issues

### 3.1 Issue: Payment Failed But Money Debited

**Symptoms:**
- Customer attempted payment, saw failure on screen, but bank account / card was charged.

**Troubleshooting Steps:**
1. Reassure customer — most such cases auto-refund within 5–7 business days.
2. Request: transaction reference number, date/time, amount, payment method, order attempt ID (if any).
3. Check payment gateway dashboard for transaction status.
4. If gateway confirms failure: refund is auto-initiated; share expected refund timeline.
5. If gateway confirms success (rare): convert to successful order and process normally.
6. If still pending after 7 business days: raise dispute with payment gateway.

**Escalate When:**
- Refund not received after 10 business days.
- Customer disputes the resolution.

---

### 3.2 Issue: UPI Payment Pending

**Symptoms:**
- UPI payment shows as "pending" in customer's UPI app.
- Order not confirmed on Dayjoy's end.

**Troubleshooting Steps:**
1. Wait 24 hours — UPI pending payments usually auto-resolve (either success or failure).
2. After 24 hours:
   - Check payment gateway for transaction status.
   - If success: process order.
   - If failure: customer can retry payment.
3. If customer's UPI app shows "success" but Dayjoy hasn't received:
   - Request UPI transaction reference (UTR).
   - Verify with payment gateway / bank.
   - If confirmed: process order; if not: refund within 5–7 business days.

---

### 3.3 Issue: EMI Conversion Failed

**Symptoms:**
- Customer selected EMI at checkout, but bank declined EMI conversion.
- Customer charged full amount.

**Troubleshooting Steps:**
1. Inform customer that the bank's EMI eligibility check failed.
2. Customer has two options:
   - Proceed with full payment (treat as regular transaction).
   - Cancel the order and retry with a different bank / payment method.
3. If customer wants EMI specifically: advise checking pre-approved EMI offers with their bank first.
4. Refund (if cancellation requested): 5–7 business days.

---

### 3.4 Issue: COD Not Available for My PIN Code

**Symptoms:**
- COD option not shown at checkout for customer's PIN code.

**Troubleshooting Steps:**
1. Explain that COD availability depends on courier partner serviceability at the PIN code.
2. Suggest prepaid alternatives: UPI, card, net banking, wallet.
3. If customer insists on COD: check if alternate address (relative/friend in serviceable area) can be used.

## 4. Product Issues

### 4.1 Issue: Product Not Working / Defective on Arrival

**Symptoms:**
- Customer received a defective or non-functional product.

**Troubleshooting Steps:**
1. Identify product category — consumable (no warranty, return only) vs. durable (warranty + return).
2. Request photos/videos of the defect.
3. For consumables: process return under "Defective Product" within return window (see `support/return-policy.md`).
4. For durables:
   - If within return window (typically 7 days): offer return for refund/replacement.
   - If outside return window but within warranty: initiate warranty claim (see `support/warranty-policy.md`).
5. For setup/installation issues (e.g., water purifier not starting):
   - Check if power / water supply is properly connected.
   - Walk through basic setup steps (user manual reference).
   - If still not working: dispatch technician (on-site service for large items).
6. Provide customer with a timeline based on the resolution path.

---

### 4.2 Issue: Allergic Reaction to a Product

**Symptoms:**
- Customer reports skin irritation, rash, or other adverse reaction after using a personal care / cosmetic product.

**Troubleshooting Steps:**
1. Express empathy and ask customer to **immediately stop using the product**.
2. Advise customer to consult a healthcare professional if symptoms are severe.
3. Collect details:
   - Product name and batch number (from packaging).
   - Date of first use.
   - Description of reaction.
   - Photos of affected area (optional).
   - Customer's allergy history (if any).
4. Initiate return/refund under "Product Not as Described" or "Adverse Reaction" category.
5. Report to Product Development team for batch investigation.
6. If pattern detected across multiple customers: initiate product recall.
7. Log incident for compliance / regulatory reporting if required.

**Escalate When:**
- Customer reports severe reaction requiring medical attention.
- Multiple reports for the same batch.

---

### 4.3 Issue: Product Expiry / Near-Expiry

**Symptoms:**
- Customer received a product that is already expired or close to expiry.

**Troubleshooting Steps:**
1. Request photos of: product packaging showing manufacturing date / expiry date / batch number.
2. Verify expiry against Dayjoy's inventory records.
3. If expired: apologize, ship replacement immediately, no return needed (dispose safely).
4. If near-expiry (within [PLACEHOLDER — typical: 3 months]):
   - If customer prefers replacement: process replacement.
   - If customer accepts near-expiry product: offer discount / store credit.
5. Log incident for warehouse quality control review.

---

### 4.4 Issue: Product Packaging Damaged

**Symptoms:**
- Product packaging is damaged but product inside appears intact.

**Troubleshooting Steps:**
1. Request photos of damaged packaging and product inside.
2. If product is intact and customer is willing to keep:
   - Offer partial refund / store credit for inconvenience.
   - Note damage in dispatch records for courier claim.
3. If product is damaged or customer wants replacement:
   - Initiate return under "Damaged in Transit" (see `support/return-policy.md`).
   - Return shipping borne by Dayjoy.

## 5. Account & Login Issues

### 5.1 Issue: Cannot Log In to Customer Portal

**Symptoms:**
- Customer cannot log in with email/phone and password.

**Troubleshooting Steps:**
1. Verify customer is using the correct login URL (Customer Portal — Phase 2).
2. Ask customer to use "Forgot Password" flow to reset.
3. Check that email / phone is correctly registered (verify with order history).
4. If account doesn't exist: ask customer to register using the email/phone used for orders.
5. If still unable: escalate to tech support for account investigation.

---

### 5.2 Issue: OTP Not Received

**Symptoms:**
- Customer trying to register / reset password but OTP not received.

**Troubleshooting Steps:**
1. Ask customer to wait 2–3 minutes (OTP delivery can be delayed).
2. Check for typos in email / phone number.
3. Request resend OTP (max 3 attempts per 10 minutes).
4. If still not received:
   - For SMS OTP: check DND status with customer's mobile operator.
   - For email OTP: check spam / promotions folder.
5. As fallback: try alternate channel (email if phone failed, or vice versa).
6. If persistent: escalate to tech support.

---

### 5.3 Issue: Account Locked

**Symptoms:**
- Customer's account is locked due to multiple failed login attempts.

**Troubleshooting Steps:**
1. Inform customer that the lock is a security measure (auto-locks after 5 failed attempts).
2. Ask customer to wait 15 minutes for auto-unlock OR
3. Initiate password reset via "Forgot Password" — this also unlocks the account.
4. If customer suspects unauthorized access: lock the account manually, investigate, and reset password with customer on call.

## 6. Distributor-Specific Issues

### 6.1 Issue: Commission Not Credited

**Symptoms:**
- Distributor's commission / incentive is not showing in their wallet or statement.

**Troubleshooting Steps:**
1. Verify the distributor's downline activity (eligible sales for commission period).
2. Check if rank advancement criteria were met for the bonus in question.
3. Confirm commission cycle dates (commissions are typically calculated weekly/monthly).
4. If commission calculation is correct but payout failed: check bank details, re-initiate payout.
5. If commission calculation is incorrect: raise ticket with Finance team for manual review.
6. Provide distributor with commission statement (PDF) for transparency.

---

### 6.2 Issue: Downline Not Showing

**Symptoms:**
- Distributor's recruited distributor not showing in their downline tree.

**Troubleshooting Steps:**
1. Verify the new distributor's sponsor ID matches the recruiting distributor's ID.
2. Check if new distributor's KYC / activation is complete (pending KYC = not active in tree).
3. Check binary placement (left vs. right leg) — distributor may need to manually place new recruit.
4. If error in sponsor ID: raise ticket for correction (rare; requires Compliance approval).

---

### 6.3 Issue: Distributor ID / Login Issues

**Symptoms:**
- Distributor cannot access the Distributor Portal.

**Troubleshooting Steps:**
1. Verify distributor ID is active (not suspended / terminated).
2. Use "Forgot Password" flow with registered email / phone.
3. If distributor ID is suspended: route to Compliance team for reinstatement review.
4. If new distributor hasn't received login credentials: check registration status, resend credentials.
