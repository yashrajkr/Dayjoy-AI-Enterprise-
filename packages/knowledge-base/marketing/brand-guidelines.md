## 1. Brand Overview

**Dayjoy** is a wellness direct selling brand operated by Dayjoy Marketing Private Limited, headquartered in Kota, Rajasthan. The brand represents **quality wellness products, fair business opportunity, and 24/7 support** to its customers and distributors across India.

The Dayjoy brand must be presented consistently across every channel — voice AI, WhatsApp, web chat, customer portal, distributor portal, social media, packaging, advertising, and offline events. This document defines the brand voice, tone, visual identity, and logo usage rules.

---

## 2. Brand Voice & Tone

### 2.1 Brand Voice

The Dayjoy brand voice is:

- **Warm and human** — We speak like a helpful, knowledgeable friend — never robotic or corporate.
- **Confident but humble** — We're proud of our products but never make exaggerated claims.
- **Clear and direct** — Plain language; no jargon; no unnecessary complexity.
- **Empathetic and respectful** — We listen first, validate concerns, then respond.
- **Compliant and truthful** — Every claim is grounded in verified facts. No hype.

### 2.2 Tone Variations by Audience

| Audience | Tone | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Customer seeking product info | Helpful, informative, friendly | "I'd be happy to help you find the right product! Could you tell me what you're looking for?" |
| Customer with complaint | Empathetic, apologetic, action-oriented | "I'm sorry to hear about your experience. Let's fix this right away — here's what I'll do." |
| Prospective distributor | Encouraging, opportunity-focused, transparent | "Welcome to Dayjoy! Becoming a distributor is free and simple. Let me walk you through it." |
| Existing distributor | Professional, supportive, recognition-oriented | "Congratulations on your rank advancement! Here are the next steps to grow your business." |
| Employee / Internal | Direct, factual, efficient | "Customer X has issue Y. Resolution: Z. Timeline: 3 business days." |
| Management | Concise, data-driven, strategic | "Q3 distributor activation at 72%, up from 65% in Q2. Top driver: new onboarding flow." |

### 2.3 What to Avoid

- ❌ Exaggerated claims ("miracle cure," "world's best," "100% guaranteed results")
- ❌ Medical claims not backed by regulatory approval
- ❌ Income claims without disclosure of average earnings
- ❌ Pressure tactics ("limited time only," "buy now or miss out")
- ❌ Comparative claims naming competitors
- ❌ Jargon, acronyms, technical terms without explanation
- ❌ Sarcasm, condescension, dismissiveness
- ❌ Religious, political, or culturally sensitive language
- ❌ Emoji overload (max 1–2 per message, only in casual contexts)

### 2.4 Language & Localization

- **Primary language:** English (Indian English conventions).
- **Secondary language:** Hindi (Devanagari script and Roman/Hinglish).
- **Phase 2:** Regional languages (Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Bengali, Marathi, Punjabi, Gujarati) — to be added iteratively.
- **AI assistants** should mirror the customer's language. If the customer speaks Hindi, respond in Hindi. If English, respond in English.

**Logo Usage Rules:**

1. **Clear space:** Maintain minimum clear space equal to the height of the "D" in "Dayjoy" around the logo.
2. **Minimum size:** 120px wide for digital, 30mm wide for print.
3. **Color variations:** Use the primary logo on light backgrounds; use the reversed (white) logo on dark backgrounds or brand-color backgrounds.
4. **Do not:**
   - Stretch, skew, or rotate the logo.
   - Change the logo colors.
   - Add effects (drop shadows, glows, gradients).
   - Place the logo on busy or low-contrast backgrounds.
   - Recreate or alter the logo.
   - Use the logo as a decorative element.

### 4.4 Iconography

- Use **Lucide icons** (already included in the Dayjoy AI Enterprise Platform admin dashboard).
- Stroke weight: 1.5px (consistent across the platform).
- Icon size: 16px, 20px, 24px, 32px, 48px (use multiples of 4).
- Color: inherit from surrounding text color; or use brand color for emphasis.

### 4.5 Imagery

- **Style:** Authentic, warm, diverse Indian people using products in real-life settings.
- **Diversity:** Reflect India's diversity (age, gender, region, ability).
- **Quality:** High-resolution; properly lit; in-focus.
- **Avoid:**
  - Stock-photo clichés (perfect models, fake smiles).
  - Cultural stereotypes.
  - Misleading product representations (e.g., oversized pills, exaggerated before/after).
  - Images of medical professionals endorsing products (unless properly licensed).

## 6. Do's and Don'ts

### 6.1 Do's

- ✅ Use the official brand logo and color palette consistently.
- ✅ Back product claims with verified research.
- ✅ Disclose average distributor earnings per Direct Selling Guidelines.
- ✅ Use Indian English conventions (e.g., "color" not "colour" is acceptable; "₹" not "$").
- ✅ Show real customers and distributors in marketing materials (with consent).
- ✅ Disclose material connections (e.g., distributor testimonials must disclose distributor status).
- ✅ Comply with ASCI (Advertising Standards Council of India) guidelines.

### 6.2 Don'ts

- ❌ Do not use exaggerated income claims ("Earn ₹1 lakh per month!").
- ❌ Do not make medical claims without regulatory approval.
- ❌ Do not use before/after photos for wellness products without substantiation.
- ❌ Do not target minors (under 18) for distributor recruitment.
- ❌ Do not use religious or political imagery.
- ❌ Do not use competitor names in marketing materials.
- ❌ Do not use high-pressure sales tactics.
- ❌ Do not alter product images to exaggerate results.

## 8. Distributor Brand Usage

Distributors may use the Dayjoy brand in their personal marketing with restrictions:

### 8.1 Allowed

- Use of Dayjoy logo on business cards (with prior approval).
- Use of official product images from the Distributor Portal.
- Identifying as an "Independent Dayjoy Distributor" on social media profiles.
- Sharing official Dayjoy marketing content.

### 8.2 Not Allowed

- Creating unofficial Dayjoy websites or social media pages.
- Modifying the logo or brand colors.
- Making income or product claims not approved by Dayjoy.
- Using the Dayjoy brand for non-Dayjoy products or services.
- Implying they are Dayjoy employees (they are independent contractors).
- Registering domain names containing "dayjoy" (other than approved).
