5. **Consent:** Signed consent form (Form T-1) obtained before use in marketing materials.

### 8.2 Distributor Success Stories

1. **Trigger:** Rank advancement event triggers outreach from Business Support.
2. **Interview:** Marketing team conducts 30-minute interview with distributor.
3. **Story drafting:** Marketing drafts story from interview; distributor reviews and approves.
4. **Photo/video:** Arranged with distributor's consent.
5. **Compliance review:** Compliance team verifies income disclosure, claims, and consent.
6. **Publication:** Story published on website, portal, social media after final approval.

## 9. Using Testimonials in AI Responses

AI assistants (Voice AI, WhatsApp AI, Web Chat) may reference testimonials when:

- Customer asks for product reviews or social proof.
- Prospective distributor asks about real distributor experiences.
- Customer is on the fence about a product.

**AI Behavior Rules:**

- Quote testimonials **verbatim** (do not paraphrase or embellish).
- Always include the consent attribution ("— Customer Name, City").
- For distributor stories, always include income disclosure ("Results not typical. Average earnings: ₹X/year.").
- Never use a testimonial to make a medical claim the testimonial itself doesn't make.
- If no relevant testimonial is available, say so honestly — do not fabricate.
