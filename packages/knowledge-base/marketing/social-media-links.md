## 3. Posting Guidelines — Marketing Team

### 3.1 Content Pillars

Dayjoy's social media content revolves around 5 pillars:

1. **Product Education** (30%) — Product benefits, usage, ingredients, FAQs.
2. **Wellness Tips** (20%) — General wellness content (not product-specific).
3. **Distributor Spotlight** (15%) — Distributor success stories (with income disclosure).
4. **Company Updates** (15%) — New product launches, awards, events, milestones.
5. **Promotional Content** (20%) — Active offers, festival campaigns, combo deals.

### 3.2 Posting Frequency

| Platform | Frequency | Best Times (IST) | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| **LinkedIn** | 3–5 posts/week | Tue–Thu, 9–11 AM | REQUIRES CLIENT INPUT |
| **Facebook** | 1–2 posts/day | 12–1 PM, 7–9 PM | REQUIRES CLIENT INPUT |
| **Instagram** | 1 post/day + 3–5 stories/day | 12–1 PM, 7–9 PM | REQUIRES CLIENT INPUT |
| **YouTube** | 1–2 videos/week | Sat–Sun, 10 AM | REQUIRES CLIENT INPUT |
| **X (Twitter)** | 3–5 tweets/day | Tue–Thu, 10 AM – 12 PM | REQUIRES CLIENT INPUT |
| **WhatsApp Status** | 1–2/day | 9 AM, 7 PM | REQUIRES CLIENT INPUT |

### 3.3 Content Formats

- **Static posts** — Single image + caption (1080×1080px).
- **Carousel posts** — Multiple images (Instagram, LinkedIn).
- **Short-form video** — Reels / Shorts (15–60 seconds).
- **Long-form video** — YouTube (5–15 minutes).
- **Stories** — 24-hour ephemeral content (Instagram, Facebook).
- **Live streams** — Monthly distributor Q&A, product launches.
- **Text posts** — LinkedIn articles, X (Twitter) threads.

### 3.4 Caption Guidelines

- **Tone:** Warm, friendly, informative (per Brand Guidelines).
- **Length:** Instagram 100–300 chars; LinkedIn 200–500 chars; YouTube description 300–1000 chars.
- **Hashtags:**
  - Always include: `#Dayjoy` `#DayjoyWellness` `#DirectSellingIndia`
  - Festival/campaign-specific: `#DiwaliWithDayjoy` `#WellnessJourney` etc.
  - Distributor-focused: `#DayjoyDistributor` `#DayjoyBusiness`
  - Max hashtags: 5–10 per post (platform-dependent).
- **Emojis:** Use sparingly (1–3 per post). Avoid overuse.
- **Links:** Use link shortener (e.g., `dayjoy.in/promo`) for tracking.
- **CTA:** Every post should have a clear call-to-action (Shop Now, Learn More, Join Free, etc.).

## 4. Content Compliance

### 4.1 Prohibited Content

- ❌ Exaggerated income claims ("Earn ₹1 lakh/month!").
- ❌ Medical claims not backed by regulatory approval.
- ❌ Before/after photos without substantiation.
- ❌ Comparative claims naming competitors.
- ❌ Religious, political, or culturally insensitive content.
- ❌ Content targeting minors (under 18) for distributor recruitment.
- ❌ Misleading product demonstrations.
- ❌ Reuse of customer photos without consent.
- ❌ Pyramid scheme implications (emphasize product sales, not recruitment).
- ❌ False scarcity ("Only 5 left!" when stock is ample).

### 4.2 Required Disclosures

- **Distributor earnings:** Any post mentioning distributor income must include "Average distributor earnings: ₹X/year. See dayjoy.in/income-disclosure. Results not typical."
- **Sponsored content:** Paid partnerships must be tagged per platform rules (#ad, #sponsored, Paid Partnership tag).
- **Material connection:** If a customer/distributor is compensated for content, disclose it.
- **Health disclaimers:** "This product is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease. Consult your healthcare professional before use."
- **Income disclosure link:** Every distributor recruitment post must link to `dayjoy.in/income-disclosure`.

### 4.3 Approval Workflow

All social media content must go through:

1. **Content creator** drafts post (copy + creative).
2. **Marketing reviewer** checks for brand alignment and tone.
3. **Compliance reviewer** checks for regulatory compliance (mandatory for health/income content).
4. **Marketing Head** approves for publishing.
5. **Scheduler** schedules via social media management tool.

> **SLA:** Standard content — 48-hour turnaround. Compliance-sensitive content — 72-hour turnaround. Crisis-response content — same-day.

## 5. Distributor Social Media Guidelines

Distributors are encouraged to promote Dayjoy products on their personal social media, with restrictions:

### 5.1 Allowed

- ✅ Post about products they personally use and recommend.
- ✅ Share official Dayjoy marketing content (with attribution).
- ✅ Identify as "Independent Dayjoy Distributor" in their bio.
- ✅ Share their personal success story (with income disclosure).
- ✅ Use product images from the Distributor Portal.
- ✅ Host Facebook Live / Instagram Live sessions to demonstrate products.

### 5.2 Not Allowed

- ❌ Create "Dayjoy" branded pages, groups, or channels (only personal profiles).
- ❌ Register domain names containing "dayjoy."
- ❌ Make income or product claims not approved by Dayjoy.
- ❌ Use the Dayjoy logo without permission.
- ❌ Imply they are Dayjoy employees.
- ❌ Use before/after photos without substantiation.
- ❌ Make medical claims.
- ❌ Run paid ads using "Dayjoy" as a keyword.
- ❌ Criticize competitors by name.
- ❌ Share customer information without consent.

### 5.3 Distributor Training on Social Media

- Distributors receive social media training in `training-material/distributor-onboarding.md` (Module 5).
- Dayjoy provides a "Distributor Marketing Kit" via the Distributor Portal with:
  - Approved product images.
  - Sample captions.
  - Compliance do's and don'ts.
  - Hashtag guide.
- Distributors who violate social media guidelines receive:
  - First offense: Warning + content removal request.
  - Second offense: Mandatory retraining.
  - Third offense: Suspension pending review.

### 6.2 Customer Engagement Rules

- Always respond politely, even to negative comments.
- Take sensitive conversations (complaints, account details) to DM or email.
- Never delete negative comments — respond publicly, resolve privately.
- Escalate serious complaints to Grievance Officer.
- Use the AI assistant for first-response where possible (Phase 2: WhatsApp AI, Web Chat).

### 6.3 Crisis Communication

In case of a social media crisis (viral negative post, product safety issue, regulatory action):

1. **Marketing Head** is notified within 1 hour.
2. **Crisis team** convened (Marketing, Compliance, Legal, CEO).
3. **Holding statement** published within 4 hours.
4. **Investigation** completed within 48 hours.
5. **Full response** published after investigation.
6. **Post-mortem** conducted within 7 days.
