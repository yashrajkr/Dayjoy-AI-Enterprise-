### 4.2 Distributor Discount Structure

Distributors do not receive "discounts" per se — they purchase at **Distributor Price (DP)**, which is typically 30% below MRP, and earn the difference as retail profit. Additional compensation comes from the multi-tier compensation plan (see `compensation-plan/distributor-system.md`).

### 4.3 Coupon Code Rules

- **Stacking:** Coupons generally cannot be stacked (only one coupon per order). [PLACEHOLDER — confirm]
- **Minimum order value:** Most coupons have a minimum order value threshold.
- **Category restrictions:** Some coupons are category-specific (e.g., "Health Care only").
- **Expiry:** Coupons have explicit expiry dates; expired coupons are auto-rejected at checkout.
- **Refund impact:** If an order with a coupon is returned, refund is for the amount actually paid (not MRP).

## 7. Promotional Compliance

### 7.1 Regulatory Compliance

All promotional materials must comply with:

- **ASCI (Advertising Standards Council of India)** guidelines.
- **Consumer Protection Act, 2019** — no misleading ads.
- **Direct Selling Guidelines** — no exaggerated income claims.
- **FSSAI regulations** (for food products).
- **CDSCO regulations** (for any products making health claims).

### 7.2 Required Disclosures

Promotional materials must include:

- **Terms & Conditions** link for every offer.
- **Income disclosure statement** for distributor recruitment promos (per Direct Selling Guidelines).
- **Expiry date** prominently displayed.
- **Exclusions** clearly listed (e.g., "Not valid on Combo Packages").
- **MRP vs. discounted price** shown clearly.

### 7.3 Prohibited Practices

- ❌ "Limited time only" pressure tactics without genuine limited stock.
- ❌ "Buy one get one free" without proper inventory planning.
- ❌ Income claims without average earnings disclosure.
- ❌ Health claims not backed by regulatory approval.
- ❌ Hidden terms (small print must be readable).
- ❌ Targeting minors with distributor recruitment promos.
