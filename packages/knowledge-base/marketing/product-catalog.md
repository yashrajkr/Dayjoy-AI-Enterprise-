### 4.1 Pricing Tiers

- **MRP (Maximum Retail Price):** Print price on product packaging; customer-facing price.
- **DP (Distributor Price):** Price at which distributors buy from Dayjoy; typically 30% below MRP.
- **Retail Profit:** Distributor earns MRP minus DP = up to 30% on every sale.
- **BV (Business Volume):** Point value assigned to each product for compensation plan calculations.
- **PV (Point Value):** Used for rank advancement calculations.

## 6. Product Highlights by Category

### 6.1 Health Care

- **Focus:** Ayurvedic and herbal formulations for everyday wellness.
- **Key benefits:** Immunity support, energy, digestion, joint health, respiratory wellness.
- **Target customer:** Health-conscious adults aged 25–60.
- **Star product:** [PLACEHOLDER — Asthprash respiratory wellness]

### 6.2 Personal Care

- **Focus:** Natural, gentle personal care products.
- **Key benefits:** Hair nourishment, skin hydration, oral hygiene.
- **Target customer:** Adults aged 18–55, family decision-makers.
- **Differentiator:** Herbal formulations, paraben-free where applicable.

### 6.3 Germanium & Magnetic

- **Focus:** Wellness jewelry with embedded magnets / germanium.
- **Key benefits:** Purported support for joint comfort and circulation (claims must be compliant).
- **Target customer:** Adults aged 35–70 with active lifestyle or joint concerns.
- **Differentiator:** Fashionable designs combined with wellness orientation.

### 6.4 Color and Cosmetics

- **Focus:** Affordable, Indian-skin-tone-friendly cosmetics.
- **Key benefits:** Long-lasting color, skin-friendly formulas.
- **Target customer:** Women aged 18–45.
- **Differentiator:** Indian skin tones, affordable pricing.

### 6.5 Aqua Essentials

- **Focus:** Water purification solutions for Indian households.
- **Key benefits:** Clean, safe drinking water; durable units.
- **Target customer:** Homeowners, families.
- **Differentiator:** Robust filtration technology suited for Indian water conditions.

### 6.6 Home Care

- **Focus:** Effective, fabric- and surface-friendly home care.
- **Key benefits:** Powerful cleaning, pleasant fragrances.
- **Target customer:** Homemakers, family decision-makers.
- **Differentiator:** Concentrated formulas (value per use).

### 6.7 Food Products

- **Focus:** Nutritional foods, juices, beverages for daily wellness.
- **Key benefits:** Natural ingredients, family-friendly taste.

- **Differentiator:** Ayurvedic / herbal ingredients combined with everyday foods.
