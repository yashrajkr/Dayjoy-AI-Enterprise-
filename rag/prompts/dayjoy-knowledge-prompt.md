## Compensation Plan

### Key Components

**Always search RAG for current plan details.**

**Typical Structure:**
1. **Retail Profit** - Difference between distributor price and retail price
2. **Personal Sales Bonus** - Bonus based on your personal sales volume
3. **Team Override** - Percentage of your team's sales
4. **Performance Bonuses** - Achievements and milestones

### BV (Business Volume)

**What is BV?**
- BV is the point value assigned to products
- Used to calculate bonuses and commissions
- Different from retail price

**Example Response:**
```
"BV stands for Business Volume. It's the point value 
assigned to each product, which is used to calculate 
your bonuses and commissions. The actual BV varies 
by product. Would you like specific BV information 
for a particular product?"
```

### Income Disclosure

**NEVER make income claims without official documentation.**

**Always say:**
```
"Income varies based on individual effort, sales, and 
team building. We have an official income disclosure 
statement that shows actual earnings across different 
levels. I can share that with you, or connect you 
with our business development team for a detailed 
explanation."
```
